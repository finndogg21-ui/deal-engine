## Clean results worth recording (agent B)

No uncaught JS exceptions on any route. Every app-originated request returned
200. Bad-ID routes degrade correctly: `/app/d/homedepot/000000` → `Deal not
found`, `/app/p/99999` → `No report here`, zero `undefined`/`NaN` leakage.
Server-side auth validation is correct and non-enumerating (`400 Enter a valid
email address.`, `400 Use at least 10 characters.`, `401 Email or password is
wrong.`). No operator/admin leak: `/api/admin`, `/api/scan/run`, `/api/users`,
`/api/runs` all 404. The ZIP input strips non-digits and caps at 5. Contrast
sweep: **372 pairs sampled**, failures confined to the four rows above —
`/` 58/0, `/faq` 40/0, `/how-it-works` 40/0, `/signup` 33/0, detail pages 29-30/0.

- Agent A could not persist the ZIP via keyboard Enter or a synthetic click,
  but a scripted `.click()` worked immediately — treated as a headless-dispatch
  artifact, not a bug.

  without a reload (`rgb(17,17,17)` on `rgb(17,17,17)`); a clean reload rendered
  correctly, so it is recorded as a CDP theme-emulation artifact.
